Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

The short version

Shaadi Planner keeps your wedding plan on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. We can't see your plan, your guest list or your budget, and we never sell or share anything about you.

What the app stores

To build your plan, the app saves the details you type in:

  • The couple's names, your wedding date, your traditions and your total budget
  • Your events, tasks, due dates, assignees and notes
  • Vendors you add, with their quotes, payments, contact details and notes
  • Budget categories, amounts and who is paying
  • Guest parties, their side and relation, headcount, optional phone number, notes and RSVPs for each event

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign in, no analytics, no advertising and no third party code. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and it doesn't send your plan anywhere.

If you tap a vendor's phone number, email or website, your device opens Phone, Mail or Safari. What happens there is between you and that vendor.

Information about your guests and vendors

Your plan will include details about other people, like guest names and phone numbers. Please only add what you need to plan. These details stay on your device with the rest of your plan. We never see them and never contact your guests or vendors.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud or a computer, your plan may be included in that backup, the same as most apps. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Beta testing with TestFlight

While Shaadi Planner is in beta, it is shared through Apple's TestFlight. TestFlight is run by Apple, and as part of the beta Apple shares some information with us, such as:

  • Your name and email address, if you were invited by email (not if you joined through a public link)
  • Which build you installed, your device model, system version and language
  • How many times the beta was opened, and crash reports
  • Any feedback, comments or screenshots you choose to send, with basic device details like battery level and connection type

We use this only to fix problems and improve the app. We don't sell it, share it or use it for marketing. Please check a screenshot before you send it, since it may show part of your plan. You can leave the beta any time in the TestFlight app, and you can email us to ask that your feedback be deleted. Learn more in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice.

Deleting your data

Your plan is yours. Deleting Shaadi Planner from your device deletes everything the app has stored. Because we never have a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Shaadi Planner is made for adults planning a wedding. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles your information, for example by adding sync or sharing with family, we will update this page and the date at the top before that change ships, and explain it in the release notes.
