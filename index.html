Questions

Where is my plan saved?
On your iPhone or iPad only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Read the privacy policy for the details.
Will I lose my plan when a new beta build arrives?
No. Updating keeps your plan. Deleting the app removes it for good, so hold on to it while you test. If a build ever needs a fresh start, we will say so clearly in the release notes first.
Can my mom and sisters see the plan too?
Not yet. Your plan lives on one device for now. Sharing with family is on the list for a later version.
My tradition isn't quite right.
Please tell us. The first version covers Punjabi Hindu, Sikh, Muslim (Pakistani and Indian), Gujarati, Tamil Hindu and Bengali Hindu weddings, and you can pick more than one for a fusion wedding. If an event or task is missing or named differently in your family, we want to hear it.
What do I need to run it?
An iPhone or iPad on iOS 27 or iPadOS 27, and the free TestFlight app from the App Store.
How do I leave the beta?
Open TestFlight, tap Shaadi Planner and tap Stop Testing, or simply delete the app.
